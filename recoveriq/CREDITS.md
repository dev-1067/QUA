# Credits

Third-party software and data used in RecoverIQ (QUASAR 5.0 Terms §03, §06).

## Data
- India state boundaries: *India states* GeoJSON by the [DataMeet India community](http://datameet.org/), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Simplified with mapshaper for the sentiment map.
- Map tiles: © [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) contributors (ODbL).
- Borrowers, loans, agents and activity history are synthetic, generated by `backend/app/seed.py`. Any resemblance to real people is coincidental.
- Self-cure model training data is synthetic, generated from documented assumptions in `backend/app/domain/ml.py`, plus outcomes recorded in the app.
